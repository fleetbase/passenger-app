> v0.1.1 ~ "Passenger's own icon and logo"

---
## Highlights

Passenger now has its own app icon and logo, in Fleetbase's colours: a taxi roof light with signal arcs on Fleetbase blue. It replaces React Native's stock Android icon and the empty iOS icon, and the web build gets a favicon.

---
## Changes

- **App icon** on iOS (every size, including the App Store's 1024 px), on Android (square, round and adaptive), and on the web as the favicon.
- **Logo** for light and dark backgrounds, now at the top of the README.
- **Source files** in `assets/brand/`, so the icons can be rendered again from one SVG.

Operators' builds still set their own icon when they're published.
